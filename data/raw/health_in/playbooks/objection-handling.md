# Sales Playbook: Objection Handling (Health Insurance)

Internal guidance for advisors. Always stay factual, never promise claim approval, and never pressure the customer.

## Objection: "I already have health cover from my employer"

Employer group cover usually ends when the employee leaves or changes the job, and the sum insured is often shared with the family and may be too low for a major hospitalisation. A personal policy stays with the customer for life and its waiting periods keep running from the first policy date, so pre-existing diseases get covered earlier than if the customer buys later. Suggest a personal policy as a base cover in addition to employer cover.

## Objection: "Health insurance is too expensive"

Indicative premiums start at about Rs. 4,200 per year for an adult aged 18 to 35 with Rs. 3 lakh sum insured, which is about Rs. 350 a month. Premiums can be paid monthly through auto-debit or UPI AutoPay. Premiums are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80D as per prevailing tax laws. The customer can start with a lower sum insured and increase it at renewal, subject to underwriting.

## Objection: "I am young and healthy, I don't need it now"

Premiums are lowest when the customer is young and healthy, and no medical tests are needed up to age 45 without pre-existing diseases. The 30-day initial and 24-month specific-illness waiting periods are completed while the customer is healthy. Accidents are covered from day one.

## Objection: "Insurance companies reject claims"

Claims are paid as per the policy terms, which are shared in writing with the customer before purchase. Most claim rejections happen because of non-disclosure of pre-existing diseases or treatment during waiting periods, so encourage honest disclosure. Cashless claims at network hospitals reduce paperwork. Never promise that a claim will definitely be approved; explain the grievance process if the customer is concerned.

## Objection: "I need to think about it" or "I need to discuss with my family"

Respect the decision. Offer to schedule a callback at a convenient time and to send the brochure and indicative premium details after the customer agrees to be contacted. Do not push for payment on the same call.

## Objection: "The waiting period is too long"

Waiting periods apply across the industry for pre-existing diseases and specific illnesses. Buying early means the waiting period ends sooner. If the customer already has a health policy with another insurer, they can port it and the completed waiting periods are credited up to the previous sum insured.

## Objection: "I don't trust insurance sold over the phone"

The customer can verify the policy details on the official Nivaran website or at a partner branch. No payment is ever taken over a voice call, and Nivaran representatives never ask for an OTP, card PIN or banking password. The policy document can be reviewed during the 30-day free-look period and cancelled if the customer is not satisfied.
