Nivaran Essential

Nivaran Essential is an individual health insurance plan that protects one adult against hospitalisation expenses.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 18 to 60 years.
  • Available to Indian residents only.
  • No pre-policy medical tests up to age 45 if no pre-existing condition is declared.

Sum insured options

Rs. 3 lakh, Rs. 5 lakh, Rs. 10 lakh or Rs. 25 lakh per policy year.

Key benefits

  • In-patient hospitalisation expenses up to the sum insured.
  • Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 90 days.
  • All day care procedures covered.
  • Room rent: single private AC room for sum insured of Rs. 5 lakh and above; for Rs. 3 lakh sum insured, room rent is limited to 1% of the sum insured per day.
  • Road ambulance charges up to Rs. 3,000 per hospitalisation.
  • No co-payment.

Waiting periods

  • Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start (accidents covered from day one).
  • Pre-existing diseases: covered after 36 months of continuous coverage.
  • Specific illnesses such as cataract, hernia and joint replacement: covered after 24 months.

Why choose Nivaran?

  • Cashless treatment at 9,800+ network hospitals across India.
  • No claim bonus of 10% of the sum insured for every claim-free year, up to 50%.
  • Pay premiums annually, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly.
  • 24x7 claims helpline: 1800-209-4400 (toll free).
