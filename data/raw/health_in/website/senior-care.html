Nivaran Senior Care

Nivaran Senior Care is designed for parents and senior citizens. Children can buy this policy for their parents and pay the premium on their behalf.

Eligibility

  • Entry age: 60 to 75 years. Lifelong renewal once issued.
  • A pre-policy medical check-up is mandatory and is arranged at the company's cost.
  • Individual or 2-member (husband and wife) floater options.

Sum insured options

Rs. 3 lakh, Rs. 5 lakh or Rs. 10 lakh per policy year.

Co-payment

A co-payment of 20% applies to every claim under Senior Care. This means the insured pays 20% of each admissible claim amount and the company pays the remaining 80%.

Key benefits

  • In-patient hospitalisation, including day care procedures.
  • Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days.
  • Domiciliary hospitalisation when the patient cannot be moved to a hospital.
  • Annual health check-up from the second policy year.

Waiting periods

  • Initial waiting period: 30 days (accidents covered from day one).
  • Pre-existing diseases such as diabetes and hypertension: covered after 36 months.
  • Specific illnesses such as cataract and joint replacement: covered after 24 months.

Why choose Nivaran?

  • Cashless treatment at 9,800+ network hospitals across India.
  • No claim bonus of 10% of the sum insured for every claim-free year, up to 50%.
  • Pay premiums annually, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly.
  • 24x7 claims helpline: 1800-209-4400 (toll free).
