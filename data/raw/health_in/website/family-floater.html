Nivaran Family Floater

Nivaran Family Floater covers your entire family under a single sum insured that any insured member can use. It is ideal for young couples and families with children.

Who can be covered

  • Up to 2 adults (self and spouse) aged 18 to 60 years at entry.
  • Up to 4 dependent children aged 91 days to 25 years.
  • Parents are not covered under this plan. Parents aged 60 to 75 can be insured under Nivaran Senior Care.
  • Lifelong renewal is available once the policy is issued.

Sum insured options

Choose a floater sum insured of Rs. 5 lakh, Rs. 10 lakh, Rs. 25 lakh or Rs. 50 lakh. The sum insured is shared by all insured family members for the policy year.

Key benefits

  • In-patient hospitalisation expenses up to the sum insured.
  • Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 90 days.
  • All day care procedures covered.
  • Single private AC room for sum insured of Rs. 5 lakh and above.
  • AYUSH treatment covered up to the sum insured.
  • Free annual health check-up for all insured adults after every claim-free year.
  • No co-payment for members below 60 years of age.

Maternity and newborn cover

Maternity expenses are covered only under Family Floater policies with a sum insured of Rs. 10 lakh or more. The benefit is Rs. 50,000 for a normal delivery and Rs. 75,000 for a caesarean delivery, after a waiting period of 24 months from the first policy start date. Newborn baby expenses are covered up to Rs. 25,000 from day one when maternity cover is active.

Waiting periods

  • Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start for all illnesses (accidents are covered from day one).
  • Pre-existing diseases: covered after 36 months of continuous coverage.
  • Specific illnesses and procedures such as cataract, hernia, kidney stones and joint replacement: covered after 24 months.

Optional add-on: Critical Illness Rider

Add the Nivaran Critical Illness Rider to receive a lump sum of Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 25 lakh on first diagnosis of any of 20 listed critical illnesses, including cancer of specified severity, heart attack, stroke and kidney failure. Entry age for the rider is 18 to 55 years. A 90-day waiting period and a 15-day survival period apply.

  • No claim bonus of 10% of the sum insured for every claim-free year, up to 50%.
  • Pay premiums annually, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly.
  • 24x7 claims helpline: 1800-209-4400 (toll free).
