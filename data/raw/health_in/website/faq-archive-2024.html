What is PED?

A PED is any condition, ailment or injury that was diagnosed by a physician, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 36 months before the policy start date. Common examples are diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disorders.

Is pre existing illness covered?

Yes. Declared pre existing illness is covered after a waiting period of 36 months of continuous coverage. You must declare all PEDs honestly in the proposal form; non-disclosure can lead to claim rejection or policy cancellation.

What is the free look period?

You get a free look period of 15 days from the date you receive the policy document. If you are not satisfied, you can cancel the policy during this period and get a refund of premium after deduction of proportionate risk premium and medical check-up costs.

How does NCB work?

For every claim-free policy year, the sum assured increases by 10% of the base sum assured, up to a maximum of 50%.

Where can I get cashless facility?

Cashless facility is available at all our empanelled hospitals. Show your Nivaran health card at the hospital insurance desk.
