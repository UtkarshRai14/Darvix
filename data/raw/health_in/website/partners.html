Grow with Nivaran: branch partnership

Banks, NBFCs and insurance marketing firms can partner with Nivaran to offer health insurance through their branches.

Branch partnership benefits

Operational, marketing, and technology support is provided to branch partners. This includes a dedicated relationship manager, co-branded marketing material, a digital point-of-sale platform for issuing policies, and claims assistance for customers sourced by the branch.

Partner eligibility

Partners must hold a valid corporate agency or insurance marketing firm licence and have at least one certified insurance specifier at each branch.

How to apply

Submit the partner enquiry form on this page. Our partnerships team responds within 5 working days.
