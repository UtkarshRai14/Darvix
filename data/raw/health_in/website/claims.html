Claims made simple

Cashless claims

Cashless treatment is available at 9,800+ network hospitals. Show your Nivaran health card or policy number at the hospital's insurance (TPA) desk. For planned hospitalisation, the hospital must send the pre-authorisation request at least 48 hours before admission. For emergency hospitalisation, the request must be sent within 24 hours of admission. Most pre-authorisation decisions are given within 1 hour, and discharge approvals within 3 hours.

Reimbursement claims

If you are treated at a non-network hospital, pay the bills and submit a reimbursement claim within 30 days of discharge. Required documents: claim form, discharge summary, original bills and payment receipts, investigation reports, prescriptions, and a cancelled cheque or bank details for payment. Claims are settled within 30 days of receiving the last required document.

Network hospitals

The list of network hospitals in your city is available on the Nivaran website and app under "Find a hospital". The network includes hospitals in all major Indian cities, including Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata and Ahmedabad.

Grievances

If you are not satisfied with a claim decision, write to the grievance cell through the website or call the toll-free helpline 1800-209-4400. Grievances are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 14 days. If unresolved, you may approach the Insurance Ombudsman.
