# Premium Payment, Grace Period, Lapse and Reinstatement

## Premium due date and payment modes

Premiums are due on the policy's modal due date shown in the policy data page. Payment modes are annual, semi-annual, quarterly or monthly. The payment mode can be changed on any policy anniversary by submitting a Change of Mode request; monthly mode requires Sinag Bank auto-debit enrollment.

## Payment channels

Premiums can be paid through any of these channels:

- Sinag Bank auto-debit from the policyholder's savings or checking account (recommended).
- Sinag Bank mobile app or online banking under Bills Payment, biller "Liwanag Life", using the 10-digit policy number as reference.
- Over the counter at any Sinag Bank branch using a bills payment slip.
- Liwanag Life customer portal using a credit or debit card.

Payments are posted to the policy within 2 banking days. An official receipt is sent by email or SMS after posting.

## Failed auto-debit

If auto-debit fails because of insufficient balance, the bank retries the debit on the 3rd and 7th day after the due date. The policyholder can also pay through any other channel during the grace period.

## Grace period

A grace period of 31 days from the due date is given for every premium after the first. The policy stays in force during the grace period. No interest or penalty is charged if the premium is paid within the grace period.

## Automatic Premium Loan

If the Automatic Premium Loan (APL) option is active and the policy has enough cash value, an unpaid premium at the end of the grace period is paid automatically as a loan against the cash value. APL loans charge interest of 8% per year and reduce the benefit if unpaid.

## Lapse

If the premium is still unpaid at the end of the grace period and APL does not apply, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy provides no life insurance coverage and riders also stop.

## Reinstatement

A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 3 years from the date of lapse by submitting a reinstatement application, evidence of insurability (a health declaration, and medical exams if required), and paying all unpaid premiums with interest of 8% per year. Reinstatement is subject to approval by Liwanag Life underwriting.

## Hardship options

Policyholders facing temporary financial difficulty can request a change to a lower payment frequency on the next anniversary, use the APL if available, or request a review of the face amount with a Liwanag financial advisor. Premium holidays or discounts are not offered.
