# Liwanag Kalinga Protect - Product Guide (Bancassurance via Sinag Bank)

Liwanag Kalinga Protect is a regular-pay life insurance plan of Liwanag Life Assurance, offered to Sinag Bank depositors through Sinag Bank branches. Premiums are paid for 10 years and coverage continues up to age 100.

## Life insurance coverage

The plan pays the face amount to the named beneficiaries if the insured dies while the policy is in force. Face amounts start at PHP 500,000. The face amount, premium and payment mode of each policy are shown in the policy data page.

## Cash value

The policy builds a guaranteed cash value starting from the end of the third policy year. The cash value can be used for the Automatic Premium Loan, a policy loan, or received as a surrender value if the policy is cancelled.

## Riders

Optional riders can be attached at purchase or on a policy anniversary, subject to underwriting:

- Accidental Death Benefit rider: pays an additional amount equal to the rider face amount if death is due to an accident.
- Critical Illness rider: pays an advance of 50% of the face amount on first diagnosis of one of 30 covered critical illnesses.
- Waiver of Premium on Disability rider: future premiums are waived if the insured becomes totally and permanently disabled before age 60.

## Beneficiaries

The policyholder names one or more beneficiaries and their share of the benefit. A revocable beneficiary can be changed by the policyholder at any time by submitting a Change of Beneficiary form at any Sinag Bank branch or Liwanag Life service center, with a valid ID. Changing an irrevocable beneficiary requires the written consent of that beneficiary. Beneficiary changes cannot be processed over a phone call.

## Coverage while premiums are unpaid

Coverage stays in force during the 31-day grace period. If the insured dies during the grace period, the benefit is paid less the unpaid premium.
