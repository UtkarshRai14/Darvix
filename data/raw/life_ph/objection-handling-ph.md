# Objection Handling Guide - Premium Reminder Calls (Philippines)

Tone: warm, respectful and unhurried. Use "po" and "opo" with all customers. Mirror the customer's language: Filipino, English or Taglish. Never shame the customer about a late payment and never threaten lapse; explain it as information.

## "Wala pa akong pera ngayon" (I do not have money right now)

Acknowledge the situation first. Explain that the policy is still covered during the 31-day grace period and there is no penalty if paid within the grace period. Ask for a target payment date within the grace period. If the customer cannot pay within the grace period, mention the Automatic Premium Loan if the policy has it, or offer a callback from a Liwanag financial advisor to review options.

Sample phrasing: "Naiintindihan ko po. Covered pa rin po kayo hanggang sa end ng grace period, at walang penalty basta mabayaran within that period. Kailan po kaya kayo makakapag-settle?"

## "Ipa-cancel ko na lang ang policy" (I want to cancel the policy)

Do not argue. Acknowledge, then explain that cancelling ends the life coverage for the family and that a new policy later may cost more because of age and health. Cancellation and surrender requests cannot be processed on a reminder call; refer the customer to a retention specialist or Liwanag financial advisor (human handoff).

## "Hindi ko naman nagagamit" (I never use it)

Life insurance protects the beneficiaries; it is not meant to be used every year. Remind the customer of the beneficiaries named in the policy only if identity has been verified. Mention that the policy builds cash value from the third policy year.

## "Bakit ako sinisingil, naka-auto-debit ako" (I am enrolled in auto-debit)

Explain that the debit can fail when the account balance is insufficient on the due date and that the bank retries on the 3rd and 7th day after the due date. Suggest keeping enough balance or paying through the Sinag Bank app.

## "Tumaas ba ang premium?" (Did my premium increase?)

The premium of Kalinga Protect is level: it does not increase during the 10-year payment period. A different amount usually means a rider was added or the payment mode changed.

## "Busy ako, tawag na lang ulit" (I am busy, call again)

Respect it. Offer a callback at the customer's preferred day and time within the grace period.
