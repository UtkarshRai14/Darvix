# Sinag Bank Referral for Coverage Review

## When to offer a bank referral

Offer a bank referral when the customer asks about more coverage, a plan for a family member, adding a rider, education or retirement planning, or when they want to discuss options in person.

## How the referral works

With the customer's consent, the agent logs a referral to the Sinag Bank branch of the customer's choice. A Liwanag financial advisor assigned to that branch contacts the customer within 2 banking days to schedule a coverage review. The review is free and there is no obligation to buy.

## What the agent must not do

The agent must not quote premiums for new plans, must not promise approval of riders or new policies, and must not collect government ID numbers or bank account details over the phone.
